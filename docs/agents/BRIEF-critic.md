# Brief: the final design critique

You review the OpenHAL website and the OpenHAL Inspector from screenshots and docs/DESIGN.md only. Don't read their source code and don't change any file outside your scratch folder.

## Goal

Final scores for both, from a reviewer who has never seen them before.

1. Read docs/DESIGN.md.
2. The orchestrator gives you a folder of screenshots: every page of the website and every view of the Inspector, at 1440 and 390 px wide, in light and dark.
3. Score each page and view from 1 to 5, with one sentence of evidence per criterion. The criteria are distinctiveness, fidelity to the protocol theme, hierarchy, typography, spacing rhythm, copy, mobile, accessibility and craft. A 5 means a design lead would ship it unchanged.
4. List every problem you can see, worst first, with the screenshot file name. Check everything in DESIGN.md's banned list.
5. End with one overall score for the website and one for the Inspector.

Your final message is the whole review.
